# Credits

Drawn vectors in `assets/drawn/` are new cut-paper illustrations. Cucumbers are tapered tubes along a bent spine (ridges, bumps in the silhouette, white scissor edge, a misregistered second color, a boiling outline). Five bends: `cuke-a` through `cuke-e`. Flying cucumbers pick one at random.

Construction follows the local libraries without copying their paths: OpenMoji's base/shade/outline groups, ira's named curves, kitbitz's stacked flats. Album covers and the right-side doll stay the original files.

- OpenMoji color/line groups: a base fill, a darker shade, then one outline stroke. Consulted `1F952.svg` (cucumber). CC BY-SA 4.0, OpenMoji — the open-source emoji. Not embedded.
- ira-illustrations: named primitives (circle, rect, polygon, short curve) with a dark stroke. Consulted `rocket.svg` and `flower.svg`. MIT. Not embedded.
- kitbitz: ground-shadow ellipse, then stacked flat shapes. Consulted interior-kit plant pots. CC0. Not embedded.
- unDraw: flat stacked fills. Consulted `recording.svg`. Not embedded.

Album covers stay the original circular files in `moortneli4-site/歌单/`.
The right-side character doll stays `moortneli4-site/人物素材/`.
Earlier bitmap traces in `assets/traced/` are unused by the page.
Fonts: ZCOOL KuaiLe, Noto Serif SC (OFL via Google Fonts).
